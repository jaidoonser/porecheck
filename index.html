    How the checking works

    Each ingredient is matched against a list of about 130 known pore-clogging (comedogenic) ingredients and their other names, such as Cocos Nucifera Oil for coconut oil or the CI numbers for red dyes.

    High means a comedogenic rating of 4–5, or an ingredient that nearly every pore-clogger list flags. Moderate means a rating of 2–3: fine for many people, but worth avoiding if your skin is acne-prone.

    Photos are read on your phone, nothing is uploaded. Small or curved print can come out a bit wrong, so glance over the text it fills in. If it struggles, your camera can copy text straight from a label (Live Text on iPhone, Google Lens on Android) and you can paste it in.

    This is a first check, not medical advice. Different clinics keep slightly different lists.
